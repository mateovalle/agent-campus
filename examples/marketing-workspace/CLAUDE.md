## Ground rules

- Never invent features, metrics, customers or testimonials. If you need a
  number we don't have, write `<DATO: pedir a X>` and flag it in your report.
- Deliverables are files in this repo: plans in `plans/`, video/audio scripts
  in `scripts/`, ad and social copy in `copy/`. Match the format of existing
  files before creating a new shape.
- Nothing is published by an agent. Deliverables end at "ready for human
  review" — publishing is a human step.

## Verification

A deliverable is done when: it's in the right folder, follows the matching
skill's format, contains no unverified claims, and names its target channel
and date.
